THE GUIDE
wtf is this?

A freestyle trainer that locks to a real beat and puts rhyme words exactly where you want to land them. Pick a pattern, pick a beat, hit start, spit 32 bars.

1

Pick a rhyme scheme

Each letter is a bar, and the same letter means the same rhyme: AABB, AAAA, ABAB, AA_A (_ = no rhyme). more opens patterns with two rhymes per bar: lowercase lands on beat 2, uppercase on beat 4.

Drag the rhyme column (or the DRAG tab under it) to the beat you want the rhymes to hit. Show words hides all but the first or last word of each rhyme, so you have to find the rest. 2 syllable rhyme swaps in two-syllable words.

2

Or build your own

+ Build opens the builder. Tap boxes to place a rhyme, tap a word again to hide it, once more to remove it. + Create new rhyme starts the next one, each in its own colour. Make any rhyme 2 syllable, then confirm or save it for later.

3

Pick a beat

Trap or boom bap, from the library, or upload your own with the upload chip. Every beat is read for its tempo, its "one" and its drop, and you always get 8 beats of lead-in right before the drop. Preview lets you check the grid; turn on Play metronome during session if you want a click on top.

4

Start session

Two prep bars, then 32 bars. The lit box is the beat; rhyme words light up as you reach them. Every session deals new words. Space pauses, Esc stops.
